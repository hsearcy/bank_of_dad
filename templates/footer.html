A little family project.

About Bank of Dad

I built Bank of Dad to help my kids learn the basics of money: spending thoughtfully, saving for something, and seeing how little amounts grow through compounding when you start young.

It’s a simple family learning tool, not a real bank. I use it to track money I owe my kids; the conversations and habits are the point. I hope it helps your family too.

How I use it

We started giving our kids an allowance because I got tired of saying no to another stuffed animal every time we went to the store. Now they can decide whether to buy it themselves or save for something else.

I give my kids $1 per year of age every 14 days, so they get a little raise each birthday. We don’t tie allowance to chores, and this has worked well for our family.

The important part is sitting down with my early elementary school kids and talking about saving and compounding. The visual helps, but once they understand multiplying by two, we can walk through real numbers: a 100% increase doubles the amount. $1 becomes $2, then $4, then $8. Seeing several doublings can be surprising and exciting.

Those high rates are teaching examples, not returns to expect from a real investment. Use amounts and rates that work for your family; the conversation matters more than the settings.

Privacy

Bank of Dad stores the names you enter, balances, allowances, savings and investments, plus transaction notes and history. It doesn’t need kids’ email addresses or birth dates.
