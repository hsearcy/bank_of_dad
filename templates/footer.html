About Bank of Dad

I built Bank of Dad to help my kids learn the basics of money: spending thoughtfully, saving for something, and seeing how little amounts grow through compounding when you start young.

It’s a simple family learning tool, not a real bank. The balances are for practice; the conversations and habits are the point. I hope it helps your family too.

Privacy

Use nicknames for your kids. Bank of Dad stores the names you enter, balances, allowances, savings and investments, plus transaction notes and history. It doesn’t need kids’ email addresses or birth dates.
