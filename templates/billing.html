Payments, privacy & parent access

Stripe hosts payment entry and stores payment information. Bank of Dad keeps your sign-in provider’s identity ID, family ID, trial dates, free-access status, Stripe customer and Checkout IDs, subscription expiry and browser sessions. It also stores the family bank data you enter: children’s display names, balances, allowances and transaction notes/history. Use nicknames; no birth dates or child emails are needed.

Google sign-in requests OpenID and the required profile scope, not email. Profile claims and tokens are discarded after validation. Your provider may require a parent email for sign-in or recovery.

A signed-in parent can use all Dad controls. Don’t share this session as a restricted child login. A paused trial or subscription never deletes your family bank.
